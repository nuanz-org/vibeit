# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Motion and brand designers, marketers, and indie creators who need living design tools (canvas/motion) they can generate from a brief, personalize with params and assets, export, and share — without writing code.

## Product Purpose

Vibeit turns a creative vision (plus optional inspiration screenshots) into a freeform-generated interactive design tool. Users control parameters, drop in their own assets, refine via chat, export PNG/video, and publish to a gallery. North-star product feel: [brik.space](https://brik.space) tool editor clarity.

## Brand Personality

Calm, craft-focused, confident — “creative director energy” without noisiness. Tool UI disappears into the task; the canvas is the star.

## Anti-references

- Dense stacked sidebars full of instructional essays and milestone badges
- Generic SaaS hero-metric dashboards
- Over-decorated glassmorphism / gradient-text product chrome
- Card grids of identical template tiles competing with the primary composer

## Design Principles

1. **Canvas is the stage** — preview gets the center and the most space.
2. **Chat creates, controls personalize** — clear column roles; never bury both in one scroll.
3. **Labels over lectures** — short UI copy; hide debug/milestone chrome from the primary path.
4. **Restrained surfaces** — soft neutrals, one accent for primary CTAs, familiar product patterns.
5. **Same shell language** — Create empty state and Studio share one playground topology.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for body and controls. Support `prefers-reduced-motion`. Keyboard-reachable composer, controls, and dialogs. Touch targets ≥44px where primary.
